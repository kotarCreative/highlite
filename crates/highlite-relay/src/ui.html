Jira issue recipe

Analyze compiled sessions with your custom prompt and the relay's default AI provider. Review and publish each draft, or opt into automatic publication.

Enabling automatic drafting sends new sessions' summary, structured bundle, and capture analysis to your AI provider. Images are opt-in; raw audio and Jira credentials are never sent to the model. Jira receives only the generated or reviewed issue, static fields, and session identity.

Recipe configuration requires a loopback-bound relay.

Automatic publishing creates a Jira issue after every successful draft, without review. Only enable this after validating the prompt and destination; it applies to manual regeneration too.
