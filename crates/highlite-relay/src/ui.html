                  What gets shared?
                  
      
                    AI receives the session summary, structured bundle, and capture analysis when
                    drafting. Raw audio and Jira credentials are never sent to the model. Jira
                    receives the generated or reviewed issue, static fields, session identity, and
                    only the capture images you allow and select for attachment. Raw audio, the
                    original capture, and private form files are never attached.
